Mission roadmap

The road to Mars 99

Phase 01
Design & Funding

Systems architecture, habitat design and the first public fundraising round for Mars 99.

Phase 02
Global Auditions

An international television interview series selects candidates for their skill, spirit and resilience.

Phase 03
Crew Training

Two years of isolation, medical and technical training for the one-way journey and settlement.

Phase 04
Launch & Settlement

The crew departs, lands and begins building the first permanent human settlement on Mars.

Why a one-way mission

Why Mars? Why now?

  • 1
    Science needs a permanent presence

    A lasting settlement turns Mars from a destination into a laboratory — geology, climate and biology studied continuously, not in short visits.

    Read NASA’s Mars exploration programme ↗

  • 2
    Technology that comes home

    Closed-loop life support, food production and extreme-environment engineering drive breakthroughs that improve life on Earth.

  • 3
    Inspiration for everyone

    A shared goal on a cosmic scale gives the world a story to follow — and shows that ordinary people can choose extraordinary futures.
